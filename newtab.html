                    Keyboard shortcuts

                    Alt+Shift+F starts, pauses or resumes a focus session from any page, and Alt+Shift+S stops it. The Focus Plus icon in the toolbar shows the minutes left and has the same controls.

                    During focus

                    While a focus session is running (including while paused), other sites' notification pop-ups are silenced so you are not interrupted. They turn back on automatically as soon as the session ends or is stopped.

                    Shows a Chrome notification when your timer finishes, even if you are on another site.

                    While a focus session is running, sites in your enabled groups (and their subdomains) show a "stay focused" page instead. Chrome will ask for extra permission the first time you turn this on. Manage the groups from the 🚫 button at the top of the dashboard.

                    Tab Stash saves your other open tabs in this window and closes them so nothing distracts you. Restore them any time below.

                      These work with the site blocker. Turn it on with Block distracting websites while focusing in the Focus tab, or the switch in the 🚫 menu.
